RUKHNAV CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about products, orders, delivery, payments and your RUKHNAV account.

Shopping & Products

How can I find a product?

Use the search bar or browse Hair Care, Skin Care, Herbal and other product categories from the RUKHNAV store.

How do I know whether a product is available?

Product availability is displayed on the product page. Stock is verified again before your order is completed.

Where can I find product ingredients and directions?

Where available, ingredients, directions, warnings and other product information are shown on the individual product page.

Orders

How do I place an order?

Add the required products to your cart, proceed to checkout, confirm your delivery information and select an available payment method.

Can I track my order?

Registered customers can review their orders from the customer account area. Tracking information is displayed when it becomes available.

Can I cancel an order?

Cancellation availability depends on the current order status. Orders that have already progressed to shipping may no longer be eligible for cancellation.

Payments

Which payment methods are available?

Available options may include Cash on Delivery, bank transfer, EasyPaisa, JazzCash and other payment methods displayed during checkout.

Is payment information secure?

RUKHNAV uses appropriate access controls and does not ask customers to share passwords or sensitive account credentials through ordinary messages.

Delivery

Where does RUKHNAV deliver?

RUKHNAV aims to deliver across Pakistan wherever supported courier service is available.

How much does delivery cost?

Applicable delivery charges are shown during checkout before you submit your order.

What happens if my parcel is damaged or incorrect?

Contact customer support promptly with your order number and, where possible, photographs or video showing the issue.

Returns & Refunds

Can cosmetics or personal-care products be returned?

Return eligibility depends on product condition, hygiene and safety requirements, the reason for return and the applicable Return & Refund Policy.

How long does a refund take?

Approved refunds are initiated after review. Final posting times may depend on your bank or payment provider.

Customer Account

Do I need an account to shop?

Available checkout options may include registered-customer and guest checkout flows depending on the current store setup.

What can I manage from my account?

Depending on eligibility, customers can manage profile information, orders, wishlist items, rewards, events, reminder preferences and reviews.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Use the password-recovery option on the customer account page and follow the verification instructions.
