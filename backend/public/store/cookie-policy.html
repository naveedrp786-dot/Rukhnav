Cookie Policy

How browser storage and similar technologies support the RUKHNAV website.

Version: 2026-08-05 Effective: 5 August 2026 Pakistan
This template is provided for the RUKHNAV website and should be reviewed by a qualified Pakistani legal professional before public launch.

1. What cookies and storage do

Cookies, local storage and session storage help remember sessions, carts, preferences, security information and website settings.

2. Essential technologies

Some storage is necessary for customer login, shopping cart, fraud prevention, security and core website functionality.

3. Analytics and marketing

Where enabled, analytics or marketing technologies may help measure performance and relevant campaigns. Appropriate consent controls should be used before non-essential technologies are deployed.

4. Your controls

You can clear or block browser storage through browser settings. Doing so may sign you out, remove saved carts or prevent parts of the website from working.

5. Changes

This policy may be updated when website technology or service providers change.
