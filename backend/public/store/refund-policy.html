Return & Refund Policy

Rules for reporting damaged, incorrect, defective or otherwise eligible products.

Version: 2026-08-05 Effective: 5 August 2026 Pakistan
This template is provided for the RUKHNAV website and should be reviewed by a qualified Pakistani legal professional before public launch.

1. Eligibility

Returns may be considered for damaged, defective, incorrect or materially misdescribed products reported within the period communicated for the order.

For hygiene and safety, opened or used cosmetics and personal-care products are generally not returnable unless defective or incorrectly supplied.

2. Condition and evidence

Keep the product, invoice, packaging and delivery materials. Photos, video, batch details or courier evidence may be requested.

3. Non-returnable situations

Change of mind after opening, improper storage, misuse, normal colour or fragrance variation, allergy or sensitivity not caused by a defect, and products outside the stated period may be ineligible.

4. Resolution

Approved claims may be resolved by replacement, store credit or refund, depending on stock, payment method, product condition and applicable law.

5. Refund timing

Approved refunds are initiated after review. Banks and payment providers control final posting times. Delivery charges may be non-refundable unless the error was ours.

6. Contact

Contact customer support with your order number before returning any item. Unauthorised parcels may not be accepted.
