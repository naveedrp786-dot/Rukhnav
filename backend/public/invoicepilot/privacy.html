InvoicePilot Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the InvoicePilot mobile application handles information when you use the app. InvoicePilot is provided by RUKHNAV.

Current app design: InvoicePilot is primarily an offline, on-device invoice and business record management application. Business records entered into the app are stored locally on the user's device unless the user chooses to export or share them.

1. Information you enter into InvoicePilot

InvoicePilot allows users to create and manage business records. Depending on how you use the app, these records may include:

  • business name and business contact information;
  • business address and tax-related business identifiers;
  • customer names and contact information;
  • customer billing addresses and notes;
  • invoice details and invoice line items;
  • payment records associated with invoices;
  • expense records; and
  • other information that you choose to enter into your business records.

2. Local storage

InvoicePilot currently stores its business records locally on the user's device. The current version does not require an InvoicePilot account or sign-in to create and manage these local records.

Users are responsible for controlling access to their device and for protecting information they enter into InvoicePilot.

3. Exporting, printing and sharing

InvoicePilot provides features that may allow users to generate, print, export or share invoices and business information. Information leaves the app through these features only when the user initiates the relevant export, printing or sharing action.

When information is shared with another application or service, the receiving application or service may process that information according to its own privacy practices.

4. Analytics and advertising

The current version of InvoicePilot does not include advertising functionality and does not include app analytics or user-tracking functionality implemented by InvoicePilot.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

The current version of InvoicePilot does not activate or process purchases for InvoicePilot Pro. If paid features or subscriptions are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate to describe the relevant processing and services.

6. Payment records entered by users

InvoicePilot may allow users to record payment information relating to their invoices, such as whether an invoice has been paid and the payment method recorded for business bookkeeping. These records are business records entered or maintained by the user. InvoicePilot does not currently operate as a bank, payment wallet, money-transfer service or payment processor.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because the current app stores business records locally, records remain on the user's device until they are deleted through the app, removed as part of app/device data management, or otherwise removed by the user.

Users should retain their own copies of important business documents and exported records where appropriate.

8. Data security

We take reasonable steps when designing InvoicePilot to limit unnecessary access to user information. However, no device, software or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

InvoicePilot is a business invoicing and record-management application and is not designed or directed specifically to children.

10. Third-party services

User-directed sharing, printing or export functions may invoke services or applications provided by the user's device operating system or by third parties selected by the user. Their handling of information is governed by their respective terms and privacy policies.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when InvoicePilot's features or data practices change. The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when appropriate.
